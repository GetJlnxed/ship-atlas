# PvP overview

The overview evaluates **conditions for a fight**, not win rates or a measured tier list. Threat examples were reviewed for 4.10.2 LIVE on 23 October 2026. Their primary community sources are linked in `src/pvp.js` and exposed in the UI. The review is deliberately version-gated: an unknown/nonmatching `observedFleetYardsPatch` disables assessments instead of reusing old meta.

`scripts/combat-data.mjs` derives `catalog.pvp` from the original hardpoint trees. The flattened field arrays must not be used to associate gun size, damage and mount. IDs are deduplicated; gimbals stay with their owning station; tractor turrets and PDC do not increase crew firepower. Extra people do not inherently add guns. One person pilots, explicitly reserved people do other work, and remaining people occupy selected independent turrets. Remote/pilot slaving separates documented capability from current-build runtime verification. Records in `data/pilot-control.json` bind to ship and station IDs, source URL, source patch and source-review date. Known capability enables an explicit checkbox; selecting it shows a persistent caveat if no matching LIVE test exists. `liveCheck` has its own build, source, date and result: verified or unavailable. Old LIVE checks fall back to known capability; current failures disable the link. Unknown mounts and legacy `pilotLinked` flags cannot enable it. The initial known records cover only the Paladin left/right wing turrets (CIG flyable Q&A, Alpha 4.3.2); no current LIVE test is claimed. Central Paladin turret and other unreviewed ships remain unknown. Turret firing arcs are not simulated.

## Armor calculation

Compare a **single projectile's damage channel** against full-health **main hull** physical/energy deflection, without shields. Multiple guns, pellets and rate of fire are not added together. Multi-pellet weapons without a verified per-projectile interpretation are unknown. Below the threshold means blocked, above means threshold passed, exact equality is conservatively a boundary. Missing, mixed or ambiguous armor values remain unknown. An incoming damage bonus can be set manually (0–100%); it affects the armor table, not the outgoing stock-loadout assessment.

This is not a component penetration or kill simulation. Shield attenuation, weak-point deflection modifiers, armor wear, explosive splash, angle, damage routing and actual crafted components can change outcomes. CIG's description of the mechanic and weak points is linked in the UI. Source records for every control weapon are linked too. Deadbolt IV is downloaded separately because it is absent from the stock snapshot; the import script refreshes it with the ship data.

## Qualitative rubric

A ship needs an active stock gun exceeding the reference hull threshold to receive a conditional assessment. Pitch and yaw >=85% of the reference constitute the interface's explicit maneuverability heuristic, not an empirical PvP breakpoint. Otherwise an occupied turret with applicable guns receives an independent defense assessment. M80 additionally compares SCM boost speed; NAV speed is not used as a promise of escape. Control and multi-ship encounters never get an automatic favorable verdict. Radar detection, acceleration, capacitor endurance, crew skill, missiles and turret arcs are unresolved, and the UI says so.

Verification covers real Hurricane, Scorpius, Cutlass, Carrack and Redeemer data, crew reservations and remote slaving, missing/concept/ground/future-patch cases, damage boundaries and craft modifiers. UI tests exercise crew switching and armor filtering.
